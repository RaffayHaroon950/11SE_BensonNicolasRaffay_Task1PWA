Quick Reviews: 5 Movies Worth Your Weekend

Short, spoiler-free notes to help you decide what’s worth watching.

Not sure what to watch this weekend? We’ve got you covered. From big sci-fi adventures to clever genre mashups, here are five films worth your time, each offering something a little different.

Benson to the Future

A surprisingly heartfelt sci-fi comedy that blends time travel with genuine character development. Its humour lands, the pacing stays tight, and it avoids the trap of over-explaining its science. A perfect pick if you want something fun but meaningful.

Kirenheimer

Ambitious, intense, and visually striking, Kirenheimer isn’t an easy watch — but it’s a rewarding one. The film leans heavily into atmosphere and tension, trusting the audience to keep up. Best enjoyed when you’re in the mood for something serious and thought-provoking.

Murder on the Aryan Cruise

A sharp, stylish mystery that knows exactly what it is. With clever twists and an ensemble cast that clearly enjoys the ride, this film delivers suspense without taking itself too seriously. Ideal for fans of classic whodunits with a modern edge.

Sountharikan: Into the Sounthverse

Bursting with creativity, this film stands out for its bold visual style and energetic storytelling. It embraces its absurdity while still delivering emotional beats that land. A great choice if you’re after something imaginative and different.

Welcome to Artquadville: Arslageddon

Chaotic, colourful, and unapologetically weird, Arslageddon won’t be for everyone — but that’s part of its charm. It thrives on creativity and spectacle, making it a fun watch for audiences who enjoy experimental storytelling.

Final Verdict

Whether you’re looking for a blockbuster sequel, a thoughtful drama, or something completely offbeat, this week’s lineup has you covered. Pick based on your mood — you can’t really go wrong.
